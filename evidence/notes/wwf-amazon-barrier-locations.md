## Question

How could dams restrict Amazon fish access to suitable habitat under climate change?

## What the study found

The combined distribution/connectivity analysis identifies connected suitable habitat along the Amazon–Solimões and projects contraction under hydropower expansion and climate change. The delivered points alone do not quantify that contraction.

## How to use the evidence

Barrier locations provide context for investigating energy-development choices and river connectivity. Assessing or ranking a dam requires the study’s connectivity and species-model outputs.

## How the evidence was obtained

Species-distribution and river-connectivity modeling

## What this dataset contributes

Barrier-location inputs to the study

## Limits and interpretation

- 562 points include 359 dams and 203 waterfalls. Natural waterfalls and anthropogenic dams have distinct interpretations.
- The future network combines current and additional future barriers; the 269 future-tagged records alone are not the complete future network.
- Connectivity indices, species-suitability results and impact rankings are absent from this layer.
- Observation and dam commissioning dates are unresolved; catalog time is a source modification date.
